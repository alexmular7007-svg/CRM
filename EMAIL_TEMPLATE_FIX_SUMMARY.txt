EMAIL CAMPAIGNS TEMPLATE ISSUE - INVESTIGATION & FIX SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ISSUE IDENTIFIED:
The Email Campaigns modal showed an empty "Template" dropdown, preventing users
from selecting templates when creating campaigns.

Root Cause: The template dropdown calls emailCampaignService.listTemplates()
which queries GET /api/workspaces/{id}/email-templates endpoint. The backend
endpoint existed and was working, but NO EMAIL TEMPLATES were seeded in the database.
