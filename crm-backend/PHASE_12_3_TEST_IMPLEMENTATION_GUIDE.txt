───────────────────────────────────────────────

   # Test with curl (replace with your form data)
   for i in {1..700}; do

       -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
       -d '{"name":"Lead'$i'","email":"lead'$i'@test.com","leadMagnetId":1}' \

Verify: ✓ Requests 1-120 succeed, ✓ Requests 121+ get 429 status
