Long Webpage for Full Page Screenshot Testing

This document is intentionally constructed with multiple distinct content blocks reaching over 4,500 pixels in vertical height to thoroughly exercise and verify full-page scrolling, tile stitching, PNG export, and multi-page PDF generation.

Section 1: Foundations of Chrome Extensions

Chrome Extensions running on Manifest V3 utilize background service workers, declarative or active permissions, and messaging architectures. When capturing screenshots beyond the viewport, standard viewport capture APIs must be combined with programmatic window scrolling.

Content continues downward across multiple vertical segments.

Section 2: Highlighting & Wikipedia Integration

Highlighting text on a webpage enables context-driven lookups. For example, theoretical physicist Albert Einstein developed the theory of relativity, which revolutionized theoretical physics and astronomy during the 20th century.

Selecting the text above and right-clicking invokes the "Search Wikipedia for \"%s\"" context menu action.

Section 3: Canvas Tile Stitching

When stitching viewport segments, each segment is captured as a PNG data URL, decoded into an image bitmap, and blitted onto a master canvas at the appropriate Y offset. Care must be taken to account for device pixel ratio and lazy content rendering delays.

Section 4: Multi-Page PDF Generation

Very long screenshots exceeding normal paper proportions cannot be legibly printed on a single standard page. The Simple PDF Writer splits tall canvases into proportionate vertical slices conforming to ISO 216 A4 ratios.

Section 5: Scroll Restoration Guarantee

A critical requirement of professional extensions is idempotence: the original user scroll position must be accurately recorded before scrolling commences and faithfully restored upon completion or error.
