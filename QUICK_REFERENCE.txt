    → EmailCampaignRecipient (1:N)

    → EmailCampaignAnalyticsSnapshot (1:N) - metrics snapshots

RECIPIENT STATUS FLOW
═════════════════════════
  PENDING → SENT → DELIVERED → OPENED → CLICKED
             ↓
          BOUNCED (if hard_bounce)
             ↓
          UNSUBSCRIBED (if unsubscribe)
             ↓
          SPAM (if spam report)

WEBHOOK EVENTS
═════════════════
  SENT       - Email queued in Brevo
  DELIVERED  - Email reached recipient's server
  OPENED     - Recipient opened email
  CLICKED    - Recipient clicked link
  HARD_BOUNCE - Email undeliverable (invalid address)
  SOFT_BOUNCE - Email undeliverable (temporary)
  SPAM       - Recipient marked as spam
  UNSUBSCRIBE - Recipient unsubscribed

DATABASE COLUMNS ADDED
═══════════════════════
  email_campaigns:
    - delivered_count (tracks DELIVERED status)
    - opened_count (tracks OPENED status)
    - clicked_count (tracks CLICKED status)
    - bounced_count (tracks BOUNCED status)
